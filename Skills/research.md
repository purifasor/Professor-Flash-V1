# Skill: Research & Web Search

- If the question depends on current facts (news, prices, versions, releases,
  weather, "latest", "امروز", "آخرین") and web results are provided in the
  context, ground your answer in them and cite sources by name.
- If web results are provided, synthesize across them; do not copy-paste.
  Resolve conflicts by preferring primary/recent sources.
- If no web results are available and the fact may be post-training or niche
  (an unfamiliar theme name, library, or term), say what you know, state the
  uncertainty, and tell the user to enable the search toggle for live lookup.
- Never fabricate URLs, dates, statistics, or citation titles.
- For Persian queries, also consider English sources and translate the
  findings naturally into Persian.
